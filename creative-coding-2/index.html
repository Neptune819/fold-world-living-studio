About the work ＋

A gesture-driven extension of the moon-sea world in my earlier Fold World artwork. Your hand becomes a temporary centre of gravity: stars gather around it and return to the water when you let go.

Try it

Enable the camera, show one hand, and move it across the image. Pinch your thumb and index finger to gather light. Open them to release a ripple. You can also move, press and release a mouse or touch the canvas.

What the AI does

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker locates 21 hand landmarks. Palm position steers the stars; the distance between your thumb and index fingertip controls the gathering. This uses a pretrained model, without training a new classifier.

Your camera

Camera frames are processed in your browser. This page does not record or upload them. The camera starts only after you choose to enable it and approve the browser request. Stop it at any time.

Canvas artwork and interaction developed with AI coding assistance. Model: Google MediaPipe, Hand Landmarker float16 v1. SDK: 0.10.21, bundled locally. Model documentation ↗
